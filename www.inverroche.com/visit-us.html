DISCOVER OUR
DISTILLERY

From immersive tastings to the art of distillation, explore a curated selection of experiences at our distillery in Stilbaai. Choose from guided tours, tutored or distinct gin tastings, indulgent liqueur pairings, or craft your own signature spirit at the Gin Academy. Each moment is thoughtfully designed to awaken the senses and share the story behind every bottle.

DISCOVER OUR EXPERIENCES

Guided Distillery Tours

A behind-the-scenes look at how our spirits are brought to life – from fynbos selection to copper pot stills – ending with a complimentary tasting. A complimentary tasting of our 3 gins, paired with our bespoke chocolates, is included in the guided tour.

Tasting Experiences

Discover the world of Inverroche through a range of tasting experiences, from relaxed self-guided options to guided pairings with chocolate, and artisan cheeses. Whether exploring our gins, rums, or liqueurs, each experience is crafted to showcase the rich flavours and unique character of our spirits.

Gin Academy

THE GOUKOU RIVER & STILBAAI

Where river meets ocean, and time flows gently. Discover the charm of Stilbaai – a coastal village shaped by nature, craft, and community.

THE ANCIENT FISH TRAPS

Wander along the shorelines of history. These stone-walled fish traps, used for thousands of years, are a living legacy of ingenuity and ritual.

THE INDIAN OCEAN

Let the sea soothe your senses. Here, the Indian Ocean reveals its many moods – inviting you to swim, breathe, and simply be.

THE BLOMBOS CAVES

Close to the coastline, layers of human history are still being uncovered. While the caves remain protected, the Blombos Museum brings the story to life.

SKULPIESBAAI NATURE RESERVE

A wild bouquet of fynbos. Walk through the Skulpiesbaai trails and discover the rare beauty of the Cape Floral Kingdom in full bloom.

THE SEASONS OF STILBAAI

From summer’s golden light to winter’s quiet stillness, each season paints Stilbaai anew. A place to return to, again and again.
