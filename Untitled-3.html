02 / Our story

02 / Our story

Built on ambition.
Driven by enterprise.

Goldrock International is a diversified enterprise with business interests across agriculture and commodities, hospitality, trade and manufacturing, and infrastructure and connectivity.

Its portfolio brings together businesses operating across different markets, products and commercial environments. From agricultural products and hospitality experiences to manufacturing and telecommunications, Goldrock's interests extend across a broad range of enterprise.

The company's story is best understood through the businesses it develops, the markets it serves and the opportunities it continues to pursue.
